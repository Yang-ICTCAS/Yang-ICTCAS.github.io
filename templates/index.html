I am an Associate Researcher and master’s student supervisor at the Ubiquitous Computing Systems Research Center, Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, working with Prof. Yiqiang Chen on generative modeling and multimodal learning. My research focuses on virtual cells and physiological systems, investigating state representations, dynamics, and intervention response prediction. I also study trustworthy collaborative learning in multi-center settings, reinforcement learning, and multi-agent collaboration, extending predictive modeling toward scientific discovery and clinical decision support. My research projects are supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the National Key R&D Program of China, the CAS Strategic Priority Research Program, the Beijing Natural Science Foundation, and the Beijing Yangfan Program, among others. My work has appeared in Cell Research, NeurIPS, AAAI, IJCAI, and IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems, among other venues. I have served on the program committees of AAAI, BIBM, and other conferences. I received the 2023 Wu Wenjun Artificial Intelligence Science and Technology Award (Second Prize in Technological Invention) from the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence. I serve as an Executive Committee Member of the CCF Technical Committee on Pervasive Computing and Secretary of the IEEE SA P2961 Working Group.
