abstract: |
  GeneCompass is a knowledge-informed cross-species foundation model built from over 120 million human and mouse single-cell transcriptomes. During pre-training, GeneCompass integrates four types of prior biological knowledge to enhance understanding of gene regulatory mechanisms in a self-supervised manner. The model deciphers universal regulatory mechanisms across species and cell types, enabling applications in gene expression prediction, cell type annotation, and drug target discovery.
