**Funding**: National Natural Science Foundation of China, Youth Science Fund (国家自然科学基金青年项目)

**Role**: Principal Investigator (负责人)

**Overview**:
This project investigates core challenges in federated learning for healthcare, where data from multiple hospitals cannot be centralized due to privacy regulations. Research focuses on:

- Handling non-IID data distributions across clinical sites
- Robust learning under label noise and annotation inconsistency
- Privacy-preserving model aggregation and gradient protection
- Personalized federated learning for patient-level adaptation
- Clinical applications: Parkinson's disease diagnosis, perioperative risk prediction, gesture-based HCI
