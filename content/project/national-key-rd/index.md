**Funding**: National Key Research and Development Program of China (国家重点研发计划)

**Role**: Sub-project Leader (子课题负责人)

**Overview**:
This project focuses on developing large-scale foundation models for life science, with an emphasis on digital cell simulation — building computational models that can simulate, predict, and explain complex cellular behaviors from multi-omics data.

Key research thrusts include:
- Cross-species single-cell foundation models (e.g., GeneCompass)
- Gene regulatory network inference using AI
- Multimodal integration of genomics, proteomics, and imaging data
- In-silico perturbation and drug response prediction
